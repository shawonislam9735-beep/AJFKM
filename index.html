Our Story

A Legacy of Islamic Education in Feni

Al Jameatul Falahia Kamil Madrasah, founded in 1978, is one of the most distinguished Islamic educational institutions in Bangladesh. With a modern education system, strict discipline, affordable costs, and highly qualified faculty, the madrasah has stood as a beacon of learning for over four decades.

Offering education up to the Kamil (highest) level, it consistently ranks #1 in Feni District for academic excellence — recognized by the Islamic Arabic University with the Best Institution Award 2026.
