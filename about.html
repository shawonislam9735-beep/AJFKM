Our History

A Legacy of Islamic Education in Feni

The renowned madrasah of Feni district, Al-Jamiatul Falahia Kamil Madrasah was founded in 1978. It is one of the most distinguished educational institutions in Feni district. With a modern education system, strict discipline, affordable costs, and highly qualified faculty, the madrasah has stood as a beacon of learning for over four decades.

Situated in Feni Sadar on its own land, the campus is naturally beautiful and ideal for students. The institution offers education up to the Kamil (highest) level and has earned an outstanding reputation in scholarship and Madrasah Board examinations — consistently ranking among the best in Bangladesh.

Our Mission

To provide quality Islamic and modern education, foster moral values, and build skilled religious scholars who can contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Vision

To be Bangladesh's leading Islamic educational institution — producing graduates who excel in both religious knowledge and contemporary learning.

Core Objectives

Why Choose Al Jameatul Falahia?

Islamic Education

Teaching the Holy Quran, Hadith, Fiqh, and Arabic literature based on traditional and modern curricula up to the Kamil level.

Modern Learning

Combining religious studies with contemporary general education to help students thrive in a changing world.

Moral Training

Building good character, honesty, and responsibility in students following Islamic principles.

Community Service

Preparing learners to guide society with knowledge, peace, and righteous behavior.
