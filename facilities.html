World-Class Facilities

A sprawling campus with 5 modern buildings, natural surroundings, and comprehensive academic infrastructure.

Academic Buildings

5 main buildings — 3 are six-storey and 2 are three-storey — providing 62,510 sq ft of floor space for academic departments, offices, and classrooms.

Modern Classrooms

Spacious, well-ventilated classrooms equipped for multimedia presentations, designed for an effective and engaging learning environment.

Central Library
Central Library

A large, well-stocked library with extensive collections of Islamic texts, academic books, journals, and research materials for all academic levels.

Science Laboratory
Science Laboratory

A fully equipped science lab supporting practical experiments in physics, chemistry, and biology for hands-on learning.

Computer Laboratory

A modern computer lab supporting digital literacy and IT education, helping students thrive in today's technology-driven world.

On-Campus Mosque

A dedicated mosque within the campus, enabling students and faculty to offer prayers and maintain religious obligations throughout the day.

Sports Playground

A large 60-decimal playground for football, cricket, and other sports — fostering teamwork, fitness, and healthy competition.

Campus Corridors

Wide, airy corridors connecting classrooms and departments, providing comfortable and safe circulation throughout the campus.
