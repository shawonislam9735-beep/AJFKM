Our Achievements

Best Institution Award — 2026

Awarded by Islamic Arabic University, Dhaka — presented to Al Jamiatul Falahia Kamil Madrasah in recognition of outstanding commitment to academic excellence.

#1 Madrasah in Feni District

Ranked #1 among all madrasahs in Feni based on verified methodology — 44 public results from 2009 to 2025. Total students passed: 9,241 / 9,513.

44th National Science & Technology Week

Students of Al Jameatul Falahia Kamil Madrasah participated and won recognition at the 44th National Science & Technology Week, showcasing their innovative "Waste Recycling Process" project.

National Science & Technology Week

Students earned certificates and accolades at the National Science & Technology Week, demonstrating exceptional talent in science and innovation on a national stage.

46th National Science & Technology Fair — 2025

Falahia students excelled at the 46th National Science & Technology Week & Science Fair 2025 (Senior Group), presenting the "Modern City and Village" project at the Upazila level, Feni Sadar.

Best Red Crescent Institute — 2024 & 2025

Al Jameatul Falahia Kamil Madrasah was recognized as the Best Red Crescent Institute for two consecutive years — 2024 and 2025 — in recognition of outstanding humanitarian service and volunteerism.
